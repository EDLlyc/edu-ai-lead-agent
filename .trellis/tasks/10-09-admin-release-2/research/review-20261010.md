## Findings not fixed in the pinned image

1. **Model-key status may claim a failed decryption was loaded.**
   `backend/app/infrastructure/model_api_keys_overlay.py:97` assigns all latest versions before
   decrypting individual keys; lines 103–108 retain the env key after a decryption failure while
   retaining that version in the heartbeat. `ModelsPage.tsx` compares those versions to claim
   the key is effective. The existing unit test
   `test_stored_keys_replace_env_keys_and_bad_ones_are_skipped` reproduces this condition.
   This does not block the initial empty-table rollout when all three consumers receive the
   same `MODEL_SECRET_KEY`, but needs a reviewed follow-up before relying on status after
   encryption/key mismatch. Reported to the coordinator; no silent pinned-image edit.
2. **The runbook understates when the first statistics sync can happen.**
   `backend/app/application/services/wechat_stats.py:198` selects every enabled unsynced account
   once it is 10:00 in the business timezone. Binding after 10:00 can trigger read-only WeChat
   statistics calls in the next loop, on the same day. The runbook's step 12 says next day.
   This is not a publication action or a release blocker; the coordinator was informed.
