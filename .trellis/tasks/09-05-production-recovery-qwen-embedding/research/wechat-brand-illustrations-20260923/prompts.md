# Image prompts

Mode: built-in `image_gen`, one generation per scene. The source character
reference was the existing Xiaosai image in the target draft. It was used only
for identity, not as an edit target.

Common specification: one 3:2 landscape illustration for a Chinese WeChat
science-education article; warm polished 3D editorial style; blue and cream
palette. Preserve Xiaosai's blue ear-shaped head, large eyes, white body, yellow
beak and central blue chest panel with the white letters `AI`. Xiaosai is a
small participant in a real activity, occupying less than one quarter of the
frame. No other readable text, labels, logos, watermark, QR code or checkerboard.

   prototypes remain in the background.

   what they see, and ask questions with Xiaosai at a science discovery table.
3. At home, a parent and child compare a surprising claim represented by
   non-text symbols on a tablet with direct observations of a small plant;
   Xiaosai encourages closer inspection with a magnifying glass.
4. Children fold and test two paper airplanes in a living room while a parent
   records non-text results and Xiaosai helps measure the flight path.
5. At a sunny home balcony, a parent and child observe beans sprouting in
   transparent jars and ice melting on a tray; Xiaosai points toward the plant.
