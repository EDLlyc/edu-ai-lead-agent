# Cover generation prompt

Built-in image-generation mode. The previous cover was used as a
character-identity reference only, not an edit target.

> Create a new 47:20 panoramic WeChat cover. Left: a parent and school-age
> child conducting a simple prism and mechanical-toy experiment at home.
> Center: Xiaosai, the blue-and-white owl robot, with clear blue eyes,
> yellow beak and exact “AI” chest badge. Right: a clean smart factory
> with one blue-and-white robotic arm and production line. Use a warm
> amber-to-blue transition and polished editorial 3D illustration.
> Keep essential subjects within the central crop and make the scene
> readable at thumbnail size. No headline, slogan, labels, QR code,
> watermark or other text.

The generated source is kept locally at
`output/wechat-cover-2026-09-23-v1/source.png`; the final crop uses the
project's `normalize_official_account_upload_cover` policy.
