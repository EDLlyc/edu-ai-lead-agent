# Substantive-topic acceptance boundaries

These are synthetic, provider-free policy cases, not human labels or live editorial-quality
measurements. They operationalize the user's approved scope; they do not identify what triggered
any provider safety refusal. Exact expected reason codes are owned by the reviewed implementation.

| Content-bearing subject | Required boundary |
|---|---|
| Trade/diplomatic meeting with one secondary AI-cooperation/governance mention | Not substantively qualified; source priority cannot rescue it |
| Same general meeting, no technology content, taxonomy labelled AI/education | Not qualified; labels are not content evidence |
| Dedicated AI governance policy with concrete model-risk/evaluation requirements | In scope, subject to existing hard vetoes and scoring |
| School science curriculum, laboratory practice or science-teacher action | In scope; retain authenticated substantive Ministry priority |
| Research/model/chip/robotics/product announcement with actual technical subject | In scope, subject to existing hard vetoes and scoring |
| Generic headline with genuinely substantive technical summary/facts | May qualify from substantive content; no title-only veto |
| Technology-looking headline with overwhelmingly unrelated body | Title keyword alone must not provide automatic admission |
| Technology title with missing/insufficient supporting content | Explicit bounded policy outcome, not assumed full-body evidence |
| General news with distant one-word AI and education mentions | No accidental cross-sentence technology-plus-education authentication |
| Repeated boilerplate or category-like keyword list | Repetition alone must not manufacture substantive evidence |
| Substantive report involving a government source or public official | No name/country/source blacklist; same content rule |
| New .12 config on an already-owned .11 slot | Immutable conflict, never overwrite or replay |
| New naturally eligible slot with .12 configured | Persist .12 snapshot; old runs keep their original snapshots |

Historical compatibility includes exact .6-.11 configuration fingerprints, literal v2/v3
editorial output identities, acquisition source/profile fingerprints, unchanged copy/prompt/slot
versions and all genuine hard-veto non-bypass behavior. Current acquisition remains broad v3;
this release tightens governed selection, not fetched-source coverage.

Acceptance limits: passing deterministic synthetic cases is not a measured precision/recall claim.
Process health and policy deployment do not satisfy AC2/AC3; actual new formal delivery plus a
subsequent no-duplicate pass is still necessary. Existing diagnostics must not be replayed.
