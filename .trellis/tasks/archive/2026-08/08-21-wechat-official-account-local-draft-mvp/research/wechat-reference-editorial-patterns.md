# Public WeChat reference: bounded editorial-pattern study

Date: 2026-08-24

Reference supplied by the user:
`https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/FaTvS15StljJ2DQSaRAHxw`

This note records aggregate structure and design observations only. It does not copy the source article, its HTML,
or its images into the repository.

## Observed shape

- Approximately 2,500 visible Chinese characters, 54 paragraph nodes and 21 image nodes.
- Median paragraph length is about 38 characters; roughly half of the paragraphs are 30 characters or shorter.
- The opening moves from a current-policy hook to several parent questions, then gives the main answer before
  expanding the analysis.
- Repeated editorial labels separate policy summary, deeper interpretation and practical implications.
- Compact summary cards convert a long policy argument into question/answer and contrast patterns.
- Images are distributed across the whole reading path rather than concentrated at the top.
- Visual emphasis is created with small areas of warm highlight color, larger dark text and generous mobile spacing.

## Original direction for this project

Learn the reading rhythm, not the expression or artwork:

1. Start with the two already-bound Ministry of Education sources and immediately surface the questions a parent is
   likely to ask.
2. Provide an original policy-at-a-glance card, then distinguish sourced facts from family interpretation.
3. Expand the current three-section draft into six bounded editorial units and keep its body in the approved
   1,800--2,600-character target.
4. Use a warm paper, deep-ink, restrained orange/teal editorial system with short paragraphs, numbered sections,
   pull quotes and action cards. Do not reproduce the reference page's red/yellow styling.
5. Reuse the three already-generated, locally inspected company-IP images byte-for-byte and rebalance their
   placement across the new article. Do not make another image, embedding, article-model or news request.
6. Export into a new immutable local directory; retain the previous bundle unchanged and keep the result pending,
   local-only and unpublished.
