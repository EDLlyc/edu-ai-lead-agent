Date: 2026-08-24

## Finding

The v2 result is safe and readable but still feels like one repeated template. Paragraph length is not the primary
gap: v2 already renders 74 paragraph nodes with a median near 32 characters, while the bounded reference study
observed 54 paragraphs with a median near 38. The material difference is visual-form diversity.

The public reference changes information form repeatedly across its reading path: opening image, questions, policy
summary, contrast statements, highlighted conclusions, section graphics and body illustrations. Its 21 image nodes
include visual separators and editorial devices as well as scene imagery. V2 has only three scene images and repeats
the same section heading, paragraph, list, quote and boundary treatments.
